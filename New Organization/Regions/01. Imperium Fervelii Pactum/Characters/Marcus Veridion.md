#Ashborn #Fervelii #Protagonist

## Background:

- Son of **Imperator Aurex Veridion**, raised in Monteverra with high expectations.
- Trained from youth in martial doctrine, empire history, and field tactics.
- Attends the **Monteverran Academy of War** — an elite institution reserved for officers-in-training and noble youth.
- Often compared to his father — seen as “the Imperator’s flame yet to be lit.”

**Personality at 18:**
- Charismatic but unsure; confident in theory but untested in reality.
- Tries to embody the discipline and strength of his father but struggles with self-doubt and moral questions.
- Deeply loyal to his comrades, though reluctant to lead unless pushed.
- Holds romanticized views of honor, glory, and the empire's role — which will be tested.

# Motive
## **Overarching Motive (Across the Entire Story)**

> **“To protect others from helplessness — the kind I once felt.”**  
> Marcus’s core drive is **to become strong enough to shield others**. At first, this is fueled by pride and the desire to live up to his lineage. But as the story unfolds, it becomes rooted in guilt, empathy, and an almost spiritual sense of justice. His downfall is realizing that power alone cannot fix a broken world — and yet he chooses sacrifice to give others a chance to live free.

## **ACT I – The Shattering of Pride**
**Act I Context:**  
At this stage, Marcus probably thinks “more” means _stronger, braver, a leader, a warrior_.  
But by the end of Act IV, “more” means _selfless, broken, wise, and willing to die for others_.

> **Motive (Act I):** “_This is where I become more than who I was”_

### Mindset / Personality:

- Starts as **idealistic, proud**, and _eager to impress_ his superiors and father.
- Believes in **honor, rank, and empire**.
- Sees war as a **rite of passage**, not a tragedy.
### Emotional Development:

- The patrol failure bruises his ego but seems survivable.
- The **eruption of Monteverra** and total loss of home breaks him emotionally.
- Enslavement turns his shame into **bitterness, fear, and survivor’s guilt**.
- Becomes **quiet, observant**, and burdened. Stops seeing himself as a soldier — just a survivor.

### Relationship with Luphera:

- Nonexistent. No divine connection yet.
- Her silence during Monteverra’s destruction will be interpreted as **abandonment**.
### Power Growth:

- Powers awaken in slavery, maybe in a moment of desperation or to protect another slave.
- Uncontrolled, raw — _fire bursts, burns his own hands_, erratic.
- Perhaps he burns a slaver and realizes what he is… and what he could become.
- As a slave in Iskarheim, he needs to hide who he is or face execution.

### Key Moral/Psychological Breaking Point:

- **Witnesses cruelty and cannot act** — maybe sees someone killed or tortured.
- Realizes he’s no different from those he failed to protect.
- **Decision:** _“Never again. If I have this power, I’ll use it to shield others — not for myself.”_

## **ACT II – The Making of a Weapon**

> **Motive (Act II):** _“If I become a weapon for the Imperium, I can stop this from ever happening again.”_

### Mindset / Personality:

- Begins cautious, wary of praise.
- Struggles to return to old roles — no longer fits the image of the Imperium’s perfect son.
- Slowly regains confidence but trades pride for **purpose**.
- Starts to see his role as **shield, not sword**.

### Emotional Development:

- Begins forming bonds again — perhaps with fellow Ashborn or old academy friends.
- Develops quiet intensity; **measured, serious**, still carrying trauma.
- Deep-seated **distrust** toward high command begins to fester.

### Relationship with Luphera:

- Begins hearing her — _whispers, warmth during training, dreams with fire_.
- She **does not command** — she **questions** him. Teaches by nudging, not ordering.
- He begins to see her as **mentor**, perhaps even maternal.

### Power Growth:

- Gains control of:
    - **Flameblade**
    - **Fire Dash**
    - **Combustion Shot**
- Learns **Ashborn discipline** — that burnout is real, and restraint is strength.
- Becomes **tactically valuable** — a rising star in the Ashborn ranks.

### Key Moral/Psychological Breaking Point:

- On the king-killing mission, he sees the **dignity and sorrow** of the Iskari ruler.
- When the king dies, **he hears the mourning of the gods** — the world feels hollow.
- **Decision:** _“This isn’t justice. This is conquest. And I’m the weapon they’re using.”_

## **ACT III – The Collapse of Hope**

> **Motive (Act III):** _“Even if I can’t save anyone, I can still make them bleed for every inch.”_

### Mindset / Personality:

- Now fully a **man of power**, feared and respected.
- But his inner peace is crumbling — caught between **duty and morality**.
- Wears a mask of control but is emotionally **isolated, burdened**.

### Emotional Development

- Tries to act against the empire’s expansion — begs senators, tries diplomacy.
- Begins to **question Luphera too**: why does she allow this?
- Starts to see the **cost** of loyalty — friends die, nations fall.
- Feels **helpless again**, just as he did as a slave.

### Relationship with Luphera:

- Luphera's voice has grown more **intimate**, more **equal**.
- She no longer feels like a remote deity — she’s a **wounded fire**, mirroring Marcus’s pain.
- The dialogue shows **shared defiance** and **shared isolation** from the systems that created them.

### Power Growth:

- Masters:
    - **Immolation Aura**
    - **Ash Pulse**
    - **Ashen Cloak**
    - **Ashlock Volley**
- May experience another **near-burnout**, this time much more powerful and mouch more dangerous; barely surviving a massive display of power.

### Key Moral/Psychological Breaking Point:

- Loses an ally, maybe someone who reminded him of his old self.
- Realizes that **he cannot win** within the system.
- **Decision:** _“This is no longer about saving empires. This is about saving people — no matter who they are.”_

## **ACT IV – The Flame That Burns Alone**

> **Motive (Act IV):** _“If no one else will can stand up… I will.”_

### **Mindset / Personality:**

- Resigned but resolved.
- Carries the **weight of memory** — of Monteverra, of the slaves, of the fallen.
- No longer questions whether he will die — only **whether it will mean something**.

### **Emotional Development:**

- Feels **closer to Luphera** again — she may weep with him, speak to him in flame.
- Sees his role clearly: not a hero, but a necessary firebreak.
- Achieves peace — in **knowing his sacrifice buys life for others**.

### **Relationship with Luphera:**

- She speaks to him one final time before the end — not as a goddess, but as a mother figure.
- Their bond is **not obedience, but mutual sorrow and hope**.

### **Power Growth:**

- Fully wields:
    - **Scorchcleave**
    - **Lance of Coals**
    - **Kindled Rebirth**
    - **Heat Sense**
    - **Ash Reaping**
- His final power use is a **controlled Kindled Rebirth** — something no Ashborn has done before.

### **Key Moral/Psychological Breaking Point:**

- Stands before the emperor — sees the man who was once his ideal.
- Sees no glory in victory, only necessity in sacrifice.
- **Decision:** _“I cannot save the world. But I can light the path for those who will.”_
