# Powers:

**Flameblade**  
The Ashborn ignites their weapon in divine fire, causing burning wounds that ignore armor. The fire reacts to emotion—rage burns hotter.

**Fire Dash / Flame Step**  
The Ashborn dashes forward in a burst of flame, leaving a scorched path and knocking enemies aside.

**Immolation Aura**  
Engulfs the Ashborn in a fire aura that damages nearby enemies over time. Can be toggled at great stamina cost.

**Combustion Shot**  
Sends out a burst of pressurized flame from the palm, like a shotgun blast.

**Ash Pulse**  
Emits a concussive shockwave of hot ash that blinds and chokes enemies. Especially effective in tight formations.

**Kindled Rebirth**  
Upon being mortally wounded, the Ashborn detonates in a last blaze of fire. Some survive, reborn in a scorched form. Used rarely—more common with Cindersworn.

**Ashen Cloak**  
Envelops self in smoke and cinders, becoming nearly invisible for a brief period. Used for ambushes or retreats. Perfect for Ashguard.

**Inferno Whip**  
A chain or flexible weapon becomes a whip of fire, useful for disarming or crowd control.

**Scorchcleave**  
A heavy downward strike with a two-handed sword causes a cone of fire to erupt forward.

**Ashlock Volley**  
Bow technique where arrows explode on impact or leave fire zones on the ground. Powered by divine ash.

**Lance of Coals**  
A spear erupts with coal-like spikes mid-thrust, burning through multiple enemies in a line.

**Heat Sense**  
The Ashborn can detect heartbeats and body heat in darkness or smoke—“seeing through fire.”

**Ash Reaping**  
After a kill, the Ashborn absorbs residual heat, healing slightly

**Fireball**
Launches a fireball

**Flamethrower**
Explodes a cone of flame out from they hand

# Mechanics:

## Power Rankings:
### **Stage I – Grafting In**

- **Selection**: Seemingly random, tied to survival of intense fire-related trauma.
- **Trigger**: Awakening often follows surviving wildfires, infernos, or eruptions.
- **Example**: _Marcus endures the eruption of a volcano and survives the ash cloud._
- **Result**: Gains unstable fire power—untrained, volatile, feared.

---
### **Stage II – Formal Recognition**

- **Requirement**: Undergo training and perform a **personal sacrifice** to Luphera.
- **Sacrifices May Include**:
    - A beloved memory
    - A name they will never speak again
    - A limb or eye
    - A symbol of identity or love
- **Outcome**: Power becomes more stable, focused, and ritualized. They are now Flamebearers.

---
### **Stage III – Institutional Power**

- **Requirement**: Make a **sacred oath directly to Luphera**, witnessed in a divine vision or ritual fire.
- **Promise Is Binding**: Often tied to loyalty, protection of the Imperium, or a vow of sacrifice.
- **Result**: Becomes eligible for Ashguard or elite commands. Their power deepens, and they burn hotter—enabled by Luphera’s favor.
---

## Growing in Power

### **Stage II – Physical Sacrifices to Luphera

> _“She does not ask for your flesh. She asks for your pain—and your willingness to be marked by it.”_

To move from unstable flame-bearer to a recognized Ashborn, the candidate must undergo a **ritual of fire**, offering pain and endurance in return for Luphera’s favor. The sacrifice always leaves a **permanent burn**, often ritualized.

---
#### Examples of Acceptable Sacrifices

- **Cauterized Wounds**  
    The Ashborn must brand or burn a part of their body—by choice. The scar remains as a sacred seal of loyalty.  
    Common locations: chest (near the heart), back of the neck, palms, forearms, spine.
    
- **Endurance of the Ember Crucible**  
    They are sealed in a stone chamber filled with volcanic heat, ash, and smoke. Must endure without passing out.  
    Survivors emerge with scorched skin, lungs, and glowing veins that flare during power use.

- **The Burning Sigil**  
    The Ashborn receives a divine brand—either by heated iron or magical flame—seared into their chest, shoulder, or back.  
    The sigil glows faintly with power and acts as a spiritual heat sink or anchor. It strengthens **resistance to Overburn**.
    
- **Breath of Cinders**  
    A ritual where they inhale a sacred ash mixture in a closed chamber. Chokes the weak. Those who endure gain a deeper connection to heat and instinct.  
    Often linked to gaining **Heat Sense** or **Ash Pulse**.
#### Tone & Symbolism

- These sacrifices are **honored**, not hidden. The scars are **ritualistic**, not grotesque.
- Many Ashborn wear them openly as a mark of pride—_“I gave pain to the flame, and it gave me power.”_
- The intensity of the ritual reflects the Ashborn’s **devotion, discipline, and control**—not self-destruction.
#### Examples of Corrupt Sacrifices

- **Cauterizing full body parts**
	The Ashborn removes part of their body and cauterizes it by choice. The remain limb or appendage becomes corrupt and burns forever.
- **Swallowing fire**
	The Ashborn ingests flaming material, a torch or a coal, and burns their insides. Their power is able to keep them alive but they are forever unable to enjoy the taste of food and drink. They are sustained by heat forever onwards; rather than being augmented by it, they are controlled and rely solely on it.

---
### **Stage III - Pacts with Luphera**

#### **The Rite of the Third Flame**

_Ceremony of Ascension to Stage III: Covenant with Luphera_

> _“You do not just carry the flame—you give yourself to it.”_

---
##### **Setting**

- The rite takes place in a **sealed chamber of blackened stone** deep within the fire temples of [[Capital City - Pactora|Pactora]] 
- The chamber is intentionally constructed to be **unbearably hot**, mimicking the presence of divine fire. **Subtle ventilation** keeps it from becoming lethal, but the heat is suffocating—designed to test the initiate’s physical and spiritual endurance.
- Present are:
    - A **priest of Luphera**, acting as ritual overseer and witness of the covenant.
    - A **small number of individuals chosen by the initiate**—comrades, mentors, or family—who silently observe from alcoves carved into the chamber’s walls. Their presence serves as both honor and burden.

---
##### **Ceremonial Steps**
1. **The Silent Entry**  
    The Ashborn enters alone, unarmed, dressed in flame-kissed robes soaked in sacred oils. Their skin quickly begins to burn from the oppressive heat. They walk a short path of scorched stone to the center, marked by a basin of living flame.
    
2. **The Ember Oath**  
    They vow to give their passion, strength, and pain in service to **Luphera and her people**—to become a sword of divine fire for the Imperium.  
    
3. **The Flamebrand Seal**  
    A ceremonial brand—wrought of black iron and lit with divine flame—is pressed to the Ashborn’s chest, back, or forehead. The searing pain marks their flesh and their soul. Their **Ember Sigil** ignites, glowing brighter and deeper than before.
    
4. **The Trial of Endurance**  
    The Ashborn must **stand within a ring of fire**, where the heat is at its most intense.  
    They must remain still and conscious until the priest declares the fire “fed.”
    - Those who endure receive **Luphera’s blessing as a divine heat sink**, greatly enhancing their fire resistance and stamina.
    - Those who falter still retain their powers but **lack Luphera’s favor**, and will burn out more easily under pressure.
    
5. **The Exit & Recognition**  
    The Ashborn exits the flame-scorched chamber, often half-collapsed, half-reborn.  
    They are now considered part of the **elite flamebound**—capable of joining the **Ashguard** or taking leadership over lesser Ashborn.  
    Those who watched the rite are never permitted to speak of what they saw—but their silence binds them forever to the one who survived.
## Power Mechanics: The Burn Within

> _“The fire does not serve you. It consumes you, slowly or all at once.”_
### Core Principles
1. **Fire is not cast. It is drawn from the soul.**  
    The Ashborn do not "use magic" in the traditional sense—they ignite their **own life force** through the pact.
    
2. **Each use burns at a cost.**  
    All powers exact a toll: in **heat**, **pain**, **exhaustion**, or even **physical decay**. Repeated use leads to **"Overburn."**
    
3. **The Pact is an agreement with Luphera.**  
    If the Ashborn uses their power too selfishly, recklessly, or without control, they risk **divine rejection—or worse, combustion.**

## The Cost of Power: “Burnload” System

You can treat this like an invisible meter that builds tension in scenes and pacing.
### 🔥 Minor Cost (Low Burn)

Used for small abilities like **Heat Sense**, **Combustion Shot**, or **Flame Step**.
- Causes heat fatigue, racing pulse, or scorched skin.
- Recoverable with rest, water, or focus.
### 🔥🔥 Moderate Cost (Mid Burn)

Used for high-impact abilities like **Scorchcleave**, **Ash Pulse**, or **Immolation Aura**.
- Causes nosebleeds, skin blistering, migraines, or organ stress.
- Can damage gear or create friendly-fire risks if not carefully controlled.
### 🔥🔥🔥 Major Cost (Overburn Risk)

Used for rare or desperate abilities like **Kindled Rebirth**, **Ashlock Volley (full power)**, or **extended Ashen Cloak**.
- The body begins to shut down. Vision blurs. Lungs fill with smoke.
- Risk of losing control and **becoming like a Cindersworn**: self-ignition, accidental detonation, or pacted madness.

## Burnout: The Final Risk

> _"The fire does not ask. It takes."_

- If a Pactorii **overuses power** or **loses emotional control**, they “burn out”—literally combusting into a explosion of flame.
- The size of the "Burnout" is dependent on the level/skill of the Ashborn
- In early-stage Ashborn (Acolytes), this is often **instant and lethal**.
- Veterans may survive but suffer lasting scars or permanent disability.

## Optional: The Ember Sigil

A branded symbol burned into the Ashborn’s skin during the pact. It **glows hotter** the more power they use—acting as a visible warning of Overburn.  
Some Ashborn keep it covered; others display it proudly, even as it smokes.


# Structure:
### **Stage I – Grafting In (Initial Emergence)**

**Status**: Unproven. Feared. Rare.  
**Military Role**: Treated as irregular auxiliaries or arcane assets.

- Ashborn are introduced as **individuals with unstable or poorly understood power**.
- Attached to legions as “assets” under direct control of a commanding officer.
- Called **"Praefecti Ignarii"** (Fire Prefects) at first—titles created to categorize the unknown.
- Often used in siege-breaking or terror operations.
- Officers are skeptical; some resent their lack of discipline or unknown loyalties.
**Notable Unit:**
- **The Cindersworn** –  
    Ashborn with poor control over their abilities. Deployed like **living firebombs**, they erupt into flame during battle, reducing everything around them—friend and foe alike—to cinders. Often treated as expendable, and feared even by their own side.

**Command Structure**:

> The Ashborn answer to a _Tribunus Ignarii_ appointed by the Senate or Emperor. This person coordinates their use but does not command them in battle directly.

---

### **Stage II – Formal Recognition**

**Status**: Respected specialists.  
**Military Role**: Integrated into formal ranks, given structure, and training.

- Ashborn now have **training academies** in [[Capital City - Pactora|Pactora]] and [[Monteverra]] to control their development.
- Rank system begins:
    - **Ashborn Acolyte** (Basic control, used for battlefield support)
    - **Ashborn Flamebearer** (Veteran, mid-tier, tactical asset)
- Commanders begin to **respect their utility**, though tensions remain with traditional Centurions.
**Notable Unit:**
- **Infernal Cohort** –  
    Small squads of trained Ashborn embedded in legions. Used for tactical fire support, breaching, and fear-based intimidation. Marked by distinct crimson-black armor and scorched standards.

---

### **Stage III – Institutional Power (Middle Era of the Story)**

**Status**: Elite and feared.  
**Military Role**: Special forces, high command, and divine strike teams.

- The most powerful Ashborn rise to become **strategic officers or elite operatives**, sometimes even bypassing traditional rank.
- The **Ashguard** is founded
- Ashborn are now viewed with awe and terror, both by the enemy and by common soldiers.
- Some Centurions chafe at being outranked by “flaming demigods.”
**Notable Unit:**
- **The Ashguard** –  
    The Emperor’s personal fire-born executioners and protectors. Called upon to extract or eliminate high-value targets with surgical ferocity. Their presence on the battlefield is both **a death sentence and a political statement**.

---

### **The Emberwatch (Separate Division)**

**Status**: Inquisitorial. Shadowy. Feared by all.  
**Role**: Oversight, loyalty enforcement, magical purity control.

- Created in response to the rise of the Ashborn and the dangers of uncontrolled divine power.
- The Emberwatch answers only to the **Emperor** and **High Priests of Luphera**.
- Investigate heresy, treason, pact corruption, or emotional instability among Ashborn or officers alike.
- Members are a mix of **devout mortals** and **psychically attuned Ashborn**, trained to detect magical deviations or disloyalty.

**Nickname**: _"The Eyes of the Flame."_
