# Powers:

**Frostbite Grasp** _(Stage I-III – The Frostmarked)_

> _“He touched the man’s throat. The skin cracked like frozen bark.”_

- **Effect:** The Frostmarked instinctively causes objects or flesh they touch to become brittle with frost. This happens involuntarily — during combat, in moments of fear, or even while sleeping.
    
- **Limitation:** Not under conscious control. May cause damage to allies or belongings. Prolonged use numbs the user’s own limbs.

**Glaciercall Axe** _(Stage II – The Frozensworn)_

> _“He hurled it into the shield wall — and the ice bloomed like death.”_

- **Effect:** Summons ice that invelopes a battle axe, heavy and humming with frost. It can be thrown with deadly force n a burst of icy mist. Each swing creates an **explosion of freezing shards** at impact.
    
- **Mechanics:**
    - Axe deals high cleaving damage and disrupts enemy formations.

- **Limitation:** Mentally taxing. Requires physical conditioning and cold focus. If overused, the weapon may splinter and freeze the user’s own arm temporarily.

**Permafrost Halo** _(Stage III – The Frozensworn)_

> _“They ringed themselves in still air. The snow would not touch them.”_

- **Effect:** Generates a **protective ring of chilled air**, slowing projectiles and freezing incoming attackers that get too close. The user becomes a still point in a swirling haze of frost.
    
- **Limitation:** Almost stationary while active. Breaks if the user moves too far.

**Ice Shard Blast** _(Stage III – The Frozensworn)_

- **Effect:** Generates a spread of stalagmites, expanding outwards from the point the Köldari slams their weapon in the ground. 
    
- **Limitation:** Saps the heat from the user each time it is used.

**Iceshard Volley**  _(Stage II - The Frozensworn)_

- **Effect:** A large icicle develops from the hands of the wielder. As it explodes away, the shard splinters. In contrast to **Ashlock Volley**  the ice shards move out forward in a straight line.

- **Limitation:** Saps the heat from the user each time it is used.
# Mechanics
## Power Rankings:
### **Stage I – The Frostmarked**

> _“They should’ve died. They didn’t. Now the cold clings to them.”_

- **Origin:**  
    The individual experiences a **brush with death through exposure to extreme cold**, often in shipwrecks, avalanches, or freezing storms. They survive the unsurvivable, marked by frostbite or internal freezing, and return changed.
    
- **Status:**  
    Not yet Köldari. They are seen as on the edge between power or death. Their presence is unsettling. Some are outcast; others become talismans or frontline warriors in raids.
	
- **Traits:**
    - Body shows signs of frostbite or necrosis.
    - Skin is unnaturally cold, pale, or veined with faint blue.
    - Ice powers manifest sporadically and without control.
    - Emotionally distant or numb.

- **Role in Society:**  
    Feared or pitied. Occasionally used as berserkers, scouts, or tokens of divine presence. Many die young. A few go on to seek the **Veinwounds** to attempt the true Trial.
### **Stage II – The Frozensworn**

> _“They walked into the cold on their own. They came back with ice in their blood.”_

- **Origin:**  
    The frozen sworn undergo no trial other than overcoming the perpetual frost in their veins. As they learn to fight the bitter cold, they learn to tame their powers. Only once they have tamed their power, can they call themselves **Frozensworn**.
    
- **Status:**  
    Respected and feared by those without the strength of ice. They now wield their ice powers with some control and are recognized as warriors of Isbjorn. Not all Icebound survive long — their minds must still adjust to the cold within.
    
- **Traits:**
    - Powers include minor freezing, frost armor, temperature manipulation.
    - Increased resistance to pain, cold, and fatigue.        
    - Eyes may reflect ice; speech may carry vapor.
    - Skin no longer shows sign of frost
        
- **Role in Society:**  
    Often raiders, duelists, or enforcers. Some serve as personal champions to jarls. Their presence lends weight to a warband or clan. While not elite, they are no longer questioned.
### **Stage III – The Icebound**

> _“They do not feel the cold. They are the cold.”_

- **Origin:**  
    The Icebound undergo the **Trial of the Ice Vein** — a self-imposed ordeal of isolation, endurance, and death-defiance; only Isbjorn to sojourn with. Only a handful survive it. Those who do are now Icebound.
    
- **Status:**  
    **Elite Köldari**, often jarls, helmsmen, or lone warlords. Other Köldari defer to them. Some are rumored to commune with Isbjorn directly in frozen visions.
    
- **Traits:**
    
    - Powers reach mythic levels: freezing blood mid-vein, summoning blizzards, forming weapons from ice, Skin can become hardwith frost.
    - They appear as normal people of Iskarheim
    - Nearly immune to cold, fatigue, or pain.
    - Few speak much. Those who do speak with heavy silence.
        
- **Role in Society:**  
    Feared, obeyed, or challenged. The Icebound are apex figures in Iskarheim. To fight them is to test one's own place in the world. To follow them is to share in their legacy.

## Power Mechanics:

### Core Principle:

> _“The icefolk dim slow — and shatter in silence.”_

Köldari powers do not draw on devotion or passion. They draw directly from **the body's own internal heat and vitality**. Each use of their abilities consumes warmth from within — not just physically, but **spiritually**, draining the user toward stillness and death.
## The Cost of Power:
### ❄️Phase 1 – Controlled Chill:

- Power use causes a **gradual drop in core temperature**.
- Köldari feel cold to the touch, but remain clear-headed and functional.
- Their skin may become pale, breath visible, and voices rasp with frost.

### ❄️❄️Phase 2 – Numbing Cold:

- Prolonged use leads to **loss of sensation in extremities**, slower movements, and sluggish speech.
- Thoughts begin to slow. Reflexes dull.
- This is where most Frozensworn stop — or collapse.

### ❄️❄️❄️Phase 3 – The Freeze Within:

- At this stage, internal organs begin to suffer. Blood thickens. Vision frosts.
- If the Köldari persists, they enter a **coma-like frozen state**, barely alive and locked in a cold sleep.
- Some never wake. Others rise days later, changed — or shattered.

## Consequences of Overuse ("Shattering")

If a Köldari pushes too far:

- **Shattering** is not explosive like Ashborn burnout. It is **internal collapse** — a freeze so complete that muscles snap, lungs crystallize, and minds fracture.
- Some Köldari suffer **permanent frostbite** or brain damage.
- Others return from the edge with strange, unnatural calm — their bodies partially dead, animated by cold alone. These are **"Wraithborn"** among legend, though many view them with fear or disgust.
### Mitigation and Mastery

Elite Köldari, especially the Icebound, learn to:

- **Cycle their powers** with periods of movement or rest to rewarm.
- **Channel heat from the environment or enemies** (e.g., standing near fires, draining warmth from foes).
- Use **discipline and emotional control** to minimize power leakage — anger or panic increases energy drain.
- **Wear minimal armor**, allowing cold to radiate outward rather than bottling it in.
# Structure

There is not a clear structure in Iskarheim. The Köldari are structed by the strongest is the head. As the Köldari grow in strength so does their military/social ranking.
