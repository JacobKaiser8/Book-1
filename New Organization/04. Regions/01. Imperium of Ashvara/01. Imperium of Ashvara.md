## Military Structure
### High Command
#### Imperator - 
Supreme Military Commander - Appointed by the Senate or the Emperor himself.
#### Legatus Legionis - 
Legion Commander - Noble born or veteran general in charge of a full legion
#### Tribunus Laticlavius -
Senior Tribune - Deputy of the Legatus: often a young noble in training

### Mid command
#### Praefectus Castrorum -
Camp Prefect - Senior veteran in charge of logistics, fortifications, and discipline
#### Tribuni Angusticlavii -
Junior Tribunes - 5 per legion - Mid-level officers aiding in administraition, supply, and command of cohorts
### Field Officers
#### Centurion -
Commander of a Century - Backbone of the military; battle hardened officers
#### Optio -
Second in command to Centurion - Handles discipline and ranks

### Regular Soldiers
#### Legionarius (Legionarii) -
Regular solider - Trained citizen-soldier
#### Immunes -
Specialist Troop - Engineers, medics, smiths, scribes - Exempt from guard duties


## Cadet Ranking System

### Year I – Initiates

New cadets. No command authority.

- **Cadet**
    
- Organized into Contubernia (squads of 8–10).
    

Purpose: discipline, physical conditioning, obedience.

---

### Year II – Proven Cadets

Basic leadership begins.

- **Decanus Cadet**  
    Leads a squad (mirrors military Decanus).
- **Signifer Cadet**  
    Standard bearer of the training century. Symbol of discipline and pride.

Purpose: early command exposure.

---

### Year III – Senior Cadets

Now mirror field officer roles on a small scale.

- **Optio Cadet**  
    Second-in-command of a training century.
- **Centurion Cadet**  
    Leads ~80–100 cadets in drills and simulated campaigns.

These ranks are earned through merit, not birth.
Purpose: command, accountability, battlefield simulation leadership.

---

### Final Year – Primus Class

Elite leadership tier. Mirrors high command.

- **Primus Cadet**  
    Top-performing cadet of the cohort. Symbolic “first spear.”
- **Tribune Cadet**  
    Oversees multiple centuries during war exercises.

Only one **Primus Cadet** per graduating class.
This would likely be the rank Marcus is competing for.
