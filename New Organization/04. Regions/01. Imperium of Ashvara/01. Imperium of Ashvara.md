## Military Structure
### High Command
#### Imperator - 
Supreme Military Commander - Appointed by the Senate or the Emperor himself.
#### Legatus Legionis - 
Legion Commander - Noble born or veteran general in charge of a full legion
#### Tribunus Laticlavius -
Senior Tribune - Deputy of the Legatus: often a young noble in training

### Mid command
#### Praefectus Castrorum -
Camp Prefect - Senior veteran in charge of logistics, fortifications, and discipline
#### Tribuni Angusticlavii -
Junior Tribunes - 5 per legion - Mid-level officers aiding in administraition, supply, and command of cohorts
### Field Officers
#### Centurion -
Commander of a Century - Backbone of the military; battle hardened officers
#### Optio -
Second in command to Centurion - Handles discipline and ranks

### Regular Soldiers
#### Legionarius (Legionarii) -
Regular solider - Trained citizen-soldier
#### Immunes -
Specialist Troop - Engineers, medics, smiths, scribes - Exempt from guard duties
