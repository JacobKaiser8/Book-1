**The Patrol Mission:**

- Assigned as a field exercise under a senior mentor or centurion — meant to simulate real command experience.
- The patrol begins mundane: guarding trade routes, patrolling mountain passes near the borderlands.
- They intercept a **small Iskari raiding party** mid raid of one of the outlying wine vineyards—cold-blooded, ruthless, and shockingly efficient.
- **Initial overconfidence** from Marcus: he tries to issue orders or take initiative based on training.
- **Reality hits**: comrades are wounded or killed, and Marcus hesitates, panics, or miscalculates.
- May suffer a **personal wound**, or worse, be saved by someone else, reinforcing his inadequacy.

**Outcome:**

- Survives, but with **shattered confidence** and a heavy burden of guilt.
- Marks a turning point: idealism burns away, and the slow forging of the real Marcus begins.
- Plants the seed of **desire to become something greater**, not for glory, but to _never feel helpless again_.
