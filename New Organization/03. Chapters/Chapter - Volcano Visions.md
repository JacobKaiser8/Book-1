Marcus awoke choking on smoke.

The air bit at his throat—hot, dry, and laced with the stench of sulfur and scorched flesh. Ash clung to his lips. He staggered upright, coughing hard, eyes watering as the world bled into focus. Black stone cracked beneath his bare feet. It was uncomfortably warm to the touch, as though some long-burning fire had just been extinguished. The usually clear coastal air was now gritty with ash. Above, slow-moving clouds the color of old bruises drifted across the sky. Lightning flickered within them menacingly but offering no light.

He looked out and saw his city. Or what was left of it. Beyond that, the ash gave way to a horizonless void. There was no sun, no moon, yet everything stood clear as if caught in a noon-day stillness. The only difference was the absence of color. There was no red, no gold, no blood. Only gray. The coastline had vanished beneath a sheet of obsidian glass. No waves. No Tides. Just a silent stillness. 

No signs of life. No screams. No weeping. Just the slow hiss of a world unmade.

He stood up out of his ash bed and surveyed the area directly around him. He was atop mount WannaHockALuggie. However, where the summit once stood, there was now a crater—vast and black, the center a roiling red of magma. The pool was small and new but the heat radiated against his face with dry intensity. He could only look down at the bubbling center for a short time before it was too much and he was forced to avert his gaze. 

He turned back to look at what was once his home. The city of Monteverra was gone. Where marble villas and sea-breeze markets once stood, there was only desolation. Smoke curled from buried ruins. The Academy—once proud, once eternal—was reduced to little more than a few half-standing pillars. He searched the skyline and found the hill where his family manor once stood. Now, not even its foundation remained. His eyes welled with tears as he remembered the last moments before he awoke—his parents holding him, pleading...

==But it was torn away==. He didn’t remember climbing here. He didn’t remember anything after the quake… after the blinding light. He looked down only now looking at himself. His tunic was unmarred. His hands were trembling and coated in soot and streaked with raw, skin as though burns had come and healed but the earlier impression had not. ==Although his skin was raw and bubbled over with the fluid filled domes==. His arms had no pain. Why couldn’t he feel the pain? Why was he the only thing spared? And what _was_ this place?

The earth shook again and instinctually Marcus reached out for something to balance him, but there was nothing to hold to. Panic gripped his chest and his legs strained to keep him upright. He heat at his back grew and turned to see the pool in the crater starting to swell. It bubbled and hissed, the level of magma crawling slowly up the sides of the bowl, once again alive and looking altogether angry.

The heat once again became unbearable but much quicker than before. This time Marcus didn't only advert his gaze but he turned back toward the ashen ruins below and began skirting down volcano’s slope. The loose ash and soot made every step treacherous as it slid out from beneath his feet. He maneuvered to avoid the glass rocks that were razor sharp as he made his way precariously down the dead mountain, or what was now a living volcano. 

Ahead, he spotted a winding path—one that was clearer and offered smoother descent. He worked his legs to both move towards it and to not fall careening down the side of the volcano. His thighs bore the brunt of the work and were burning with excretion much more immediate than what he was used to. His worked to mimic his technique he would use to run through what used to be the local hillside vineyards. Lean into the slope, let gravity pull you but catch your self just before falling. This time though he wouldn't be shoveling fistfuls of perfectly plump, ripe grapes into his face. 

Remembering what once was, he lost focus. The ashy ground was not the same as the red clay he was used to.  As he stepped onto the path, desperate for a stable way down the hill, he stumbled. As his boot landed he shifted his weight to his front leg, but his foot slipped out from under him. He wasn't able to see the slick obsidian glass hidden by the fresh ash deposited on its surface.

He crashed hard onto his back, the air punched from his lungs as he stared helplessly up at the opening of the volcano. The pool within had reached the rim. The popping and roiling liquid within was leaking out the top. The toiling surface was spitting the scalding liquid rock all over. 

Then came the climax. Then came the roar.

A thunderous explosion shattered the silence, followed by convulsing tremors that rippled through the world. The sky filled with shrieking trails of fire—molten shards ejected from the crater like comets. They flew outward in all directions, hurtling across the darkened sky toward the distant sprawl of Ashvara. Some slammed into cities on the horizon; others, Marcus watched— collided into the remains of Monteverra.

A great boom split the air, and a column of lava erupted over the rim of the volcano like a red, world-ending geyser. The shockwave that followed cracked the air, but Marcus barely heard it. Time slowed. The roar dulled. He could only watch as the end rose into the world.

The molten wave arced high into the sky, stretching wider, higher—unnatural in its stillness. Then, within it, he saw two shapes forming near the center. Orbs. Darker sections swirled within them, shifting into form.

Eyes.

Massive, inhuman, and impossibly aware. As he watched, the rest of the head formed. It looked animal, lupine in shape. 

The eyes continued to stare out over the broken land observing the majesty of their destruction. They peered out over the black wasteland, the ruined homes. Scanning through their new domain, they spotted him laying there helplessly. The developing head locked its hungry eyes onto _him_. The prey had been found.

His heart lurched and the world snapped back into motion.

The torrent of magma came crashing down toward him.

The maw of molten light and fury opened up to swallowed him whole.
