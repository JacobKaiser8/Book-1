"The Ash Eyed Wolf"

### **Element:** 
Fire
### **Form:** 
A massive, fire-aligned she-wolf wreathed in embers and smoke. Her fur glows like coals in places, her eyes burn with pale flame, and her presence radiates heat and passion.
### **Region:** 
[[01. Imperium of Ashvara|Imperium Fervelii Pactum]] 
### **Role:** 
Luphera is the newest deity, born during the early events of the story, and she emerges as a symbol of transformation for the Imperium Fervelii Pactum. The empire was forged through mortal strength, strategy, and the iron will of its people—but Luphera's arrival marks the moment when fire itself bends to imperial command. Pacts with her grant elemental fire abilities, becoming a sacred rite and a potent weapon that elevates the Imperium from a rising power to a force of near-mythic dominance. Over time, these pacts form the spiritual and martial bedrock of a new era—one defined not just by conquest, but by divine fire.

At first, Luphera is revered as a symbol of national pride and divine favor. She is the embodiment of passion, strength, and the bond between citizen and empire. Her mountain, Monteverra, looms over the coastal city of the same name and is sacred ground, believed to be the place of her emergence. Over time, however, her image is co-opted and twisted by the ambitions of the empire. She becomes viewed less as a sacred being and more as a divine weapon—a tool of conquest in the empire’s campaign to dominate the known world.
### **Character:** 
Luphera is fierce, proud, and wild. Her power is still raw and not fully controlled, even by those who serve her. She represents not just fire's destruction, but also its life-giving warmth and passionate drive. She emboldens those who follow her, igniting courage, loyalty, and ambition. But her pride and volatility mean she does not tolerate weakness or indecision. She favors those who act with conviction, but she is not a mindless force of war—she desires purpose in power.
### **Symbolism:** 
Passion, rebellion, identity, untamed divine energy
