"The Ice bear"

### **Element:** 
Ice
### **Form:** 
A Large Ice Polar Bear
### **Region:** 
[[02. Kingdom of Iskarheim|Kingdom of Iskarheim]]
### **Role:** 
Isbjorn is revered not merely as a deity, but as the highest of kings among his people — a fellow warrior rather than a distant god. He is the silent strength behind the longships, the storm in the sails, and the frost in the blood of Iskarheim’s raiders. His presence is felt in the cold winds and icy tides, though he does not govern them. Signs from nature are interpreted as his judgment or blessing.  
Pacts with Isbjorn are earned through acts of strength, survival, and conquest. He does not bestow his power lightly. Those who prove themselves in the harshest trials — brutal winters, sea raids, or hand-to-claw combat — may gain his favor and the elemental power of ice.
### **Character:** 
Isbjorn is vengeful, proud, and honors resilience above all. He respects strength, not loyalty. He is not nurturing or protective; he is a force to be earned and feared. While he values those who rise above their suffering and defend their kin, he offers no comfort — only the expectation that they endure. His wisdom is ancient, primal, and wordless. He does not meddle in mortal affairs unless provoked or challenged. Those who face him must do so with full intent to survive — or die worthy.
### **Symbolism:** 
The frozen breath of battle, scars carved in ice, the weight of legacy, vengeance with purpose, the silence before the storm, endurance beneath burden, the might of winter’s bite, a crown of frost upon the worthy.
