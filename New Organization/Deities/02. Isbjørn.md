"The Ice bear"

### **Element:** 
Ice
### **Form:** 
A Large Ice Polar Bear
### **Region:** 
[[02. Kingdom of Iskarheim|Kingdom of Iskarheim]]
### **Role:** 
Isbjorn serves as a **highest king figure** to the people of Iskarheim. They **draw upon him for strength** in battle and look to him as the divine representation of their values. Though he does not control the weather, natural phenomena like snowstorms or freezing winds are interpreted as signs of his favor or wrath.
### **Character:** 
Vengeful, powerful, and driven by a respect for strength. He sees his people as **fellow warriors**, not subjects, and values personal might and resilience above all.
### **Symbolism:** 
Strength, honor, warrior code, cold fury, divine kinship
