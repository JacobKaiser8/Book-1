"The Ice bear"

### **Element:** 
Fire
### **Form:** 
A female wolf
### **Region:** 
[[02. Kingdom of Iskarheim|Kingdom of Iskarheim]]
### **Role:** 
Luphera is the **source of power** for the empire’s citizens. Pacts formed with her grant access to fire-based abilities, giving Fervel an edge in a world where rival nations draw on their own elemental deities. Born during the early events of the story, she is at first revered with awe—but as the empire expands and becomes more militarized, she is increasingly viewed as a **tool for conquest** rather than a sacred being.
### **Character:** 
Likely fierce, proud, possibly seen as both protector and destroyer.
### **Symbolism:** 
Passion, strength, and unity through pact. Her eyes of ash hint at divine judgment or apocalyptic vision.
