"The Breaker of Skies"

### **Element:** 
Thunder
### **Form:** 
A Male Lion
### **Region/Empire:** 
[[03. The Plains of the Vaelori|Vaelori]]
### **Role:** 
Kaplakhan is a distant and aloof god, more force of nature than guiding figure. He does not watch over the clans like a protector, nor does he guide them like a teacher. Instead, he embodies chaos and destruction, a primal test of strength and survival. Most of the Vaelori clans do not worship him directly—instead, they live around his presence.

Worship is rare and feared. Those who do seek him out are typically **dark shamans**, fringe mystics who engage in bloodletting, bodily sacrifice, or spiritual trials to attract his attention. Their goal is not to please him but to draw a sliver of his terrible power into themselves. Kaplakhan’s followers are few, fragmented, and often viewed with suspicion by other clans.
### **Character:** 
Kaplakhan is cunning, brutal, and untamed. Unlike the other deities who may have kingdoms, temples, or priests, he is wild and impossible to predict. He takes no interest in dominion or balance—only in **destruction**, **shock**, and **the raw chaos of nature unleashed**. His fury is not evil in a moral sense but cataclysmic in scale and indifferent in its wake. He does not value life, loyalty, or legacy. He exists to unravel and overwhelm.

He is also unique in that he bears **hatred toward the other gods**, particularly the elder ones. His ambition lies in the destruction of divine order itself. Kaplakhan seeks to **kill the other deities**, not for conquest, but to return the world to a primal, storm-wracked state. This makes him the **primary deific antagonist** in the story's broader arc.
### **Symbolism:** 
The crack of doom, uncontrolled fury, divine rebellion, chaos unbound, storm-born destruction, the fang in the lightning, predator of gods, the sky’s scream.
