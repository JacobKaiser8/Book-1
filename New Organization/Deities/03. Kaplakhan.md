"The Breaker of Skies"

### **Element:** 
Thunder
### **Form:** 
A Male Lion
### **Region/Empire:** 
[[03. The Plains of the Vaelori|Vaelori]]
### **Role:** 
Kaplakhan is **aloof and disconnected** from the people of Vaelori, much like the fragmented nature of the nomadic clans who inhabit the region. There is no central kingdom, and worship of him is **rare and feared**. Those who do follow him tend to be **dark shamans**, practicing **bodily harm or blood sacrifice** in an attempt to earn his favor. He is not a deity of guidance, but a force to be appeased.
### **Character:** 
Kaplakhan is **cunning, brutal, and destructive**, acting as the **main deific antagonist** in the story. His goal is **not conquest, but annihilation**—including the killing of other deities. He is primal and violent, a force of chaos rather than order. His destructive nature contrasts sharply with the more empowering or guiding deities like Luphera and Isbjorn.
### **Symbolism:** 
Chaos, destruction, divine wrath, storm-born anarchy
