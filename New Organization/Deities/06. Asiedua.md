"The Verdant Mother"

### **Element:** 
Nature; Animals, growth, weather
### **Form:** 
A towering, divine woman with **tree-like features**. Her **hair flows in thick vines**, her **skin is dark like rich bark**, and her **eyes glow with verdant green** — the light of living forests. Her garments are woven from **leaves, moss, and blossoms**, ever-changing with the seasons. When she walks, the ground blooms beneath her feet, and animals are drawn to her presence in reverent silence.
### **Region/Empire:** 
[[06. Ahyɛase|Ahyɛase]]
### **Role:** 
Asiedua is the **primordial source** of all life and magic — the mother of the elemental gods. But over the countless centuries, her name has faded from the memory of most of the world. She is remembered only in whispers, half-truths, or ancient myths. To the people of Ahyɛase, however, she is a living presence — the breath in the wind, the guardian of the forest, the mother of all things.

Her followers live in a **secluded paradise**, a **Garden of Eden-like region**, untouched by conquest or corruption. There are no temples to her, only groves, waterfalls, and animal sanctuaries where her essence is felt most strongly.

**Pacts with Asiedua require no ritual or plea.** They are granted through **complete surrender to nature** — a yielding of the self to the rhythms and will of the world. When a person ceases to act with ego and learns to listen instead of ask, Asiedua welcomes them into harmony. Her gifts manifest not as domination over nature, but unity with it: the ability to **speak with animals**, **encourage plant growth**, or **call rain through song**.
### **Character:** 
Asiedua is not a god of might or miracles — she is **ancient, gentle, and fragile**, like the last bloom of a sacred tree. She is the **first breath of the world**, a mother whose strength lies not in power, but in **patience, memory, and the quiet grace of survival**.

Time has diminished her reach. Her children — the elemental deities — grew powerful and drew the world’s worship away from her. Now, her influence lies only within **the untouched groves of Ahyɛase**, where she is still honored in silence and song. She is no longer a force who can change the world — only a reminder of what the world once was.

She **asks for nothing**, demands nothing, and gives only what nature itself allows. To those who encounter her, the experience is humbling. Her presence inspires **absolute reverence**, not because of grandeur, but because of **how sacred and vulnerable she has become** — a living symbol of balance, memory, and lost innocence.

Her sorrow is deep, but she never turns bitter. Even in the face of oblivion, she remains **a mother to all**, forgiving and kind, though each act of destruction in the world causes her to wither just a little more. Her strength is in her stillness — and in the loyalty of those few who still remember her name.
### **Symbolism:** 
The breath of first life, the whisper in leaves, forgotten roots, sacred stillness, balance without command, the mother of gods, the bloom before the storm, wisdom lost to time, the fragility of reverence, the seed that remembers.
