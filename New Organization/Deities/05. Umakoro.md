"The Stoneback Guardian"

### **Element:** 
Stone/EArth
### **Form:** 
A Male Silverback Gorilla
### **Region/Empire:** 
[[05. Taurima|Taurima]]
### **Role:** 
Umakoro is the unshakable guardian and ancestral patriarch of Taurima. He represents endurance, tradition, and the power to withstand all trials. To his people, he is both protector and builder—believed to have shaped the central stone hill from which their great temple is carved, as well as the natural wall formations that encircle their capital city. His divine strength is etched into the very bones of their homeland.

Those who form pacts with Umakoro gain elemental earth abilities rooted in strength of body and spirit—manifesting as enhanced durability, stone manipulation, and immovable resolve. Power is not given freely; it must be earned through acts of **responsibility, perseverance, and the protection of community and heritage**. Pact rituals often involve **feats of strength or physical endurance** that demonstrate not only power, but the willingness to use it in service of others.
### **Character:** 
Umakoro is loud but firm, stubborn as the stone he embodies. He favors **action over speech**, and his presence is often felt in the rumble of the earth or the echoing crack of stone breaking under force. Though boisterous in expression, his **wisdom is ancient and rooted in experience**—the kind earned through generations of watching, building, and enduring.

He offers guidance not through soft words but through **pressure, hardship, and the enduring silence of stone**. He is not cruel, but he allows suffering when it teaches strength and resilience. He respects those who are **diligent and stalwart**, who carry burdens for others, hold their ground, and never abandon their people. While not a warmonger, Umakoro will fight when provoked beyond patience—his fury, when awakened, is like an avalanche: sudden, overwhelming, and final.
### **Symbolism:** 
The roar of stone, unyielding will, ancestral burden, the weight of duty, strength through hardship, carved legacy, the foundation that resists time, the voice that shakes mountains.
