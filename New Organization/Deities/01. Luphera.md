"The Ash Eyed Wolf"

### **Element:** 
Fire
### **Form:** 
A female wolf
### **Region:** 
[[01. Imperium Fervelii Pactum |Imperium Fervelii Pactum]] 
### **Role:** 
The **main character’s patron deity** and symbol of the empire’s unity through elemental pacts. She is **newly born during the beginning of the story**, making her the **newest deity**.
### **Character:** 
Fierce, proud, and emotionally potent. Her **wild, uncontrolled fire** reflects both the promise and volatility of new power. She **emboldens** her people with passion and strength, but her force is still raw and developing.
### **Symbolism:** 
Passion, rebellion, identity, untamed divine energy
