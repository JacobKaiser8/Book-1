"The One Who Sees Above"

### **Element:** 
Air
### **Form:** 
A Female Owl
### **Region/Empire:** 
[[04. The Libraries of Fushinkyo|Fushinkyo]]
### **Role:** 
Kyokaku serves as the **lead scholar and eternal curator of wisdom**—a divine archivist who watches from above and guides the people of Fushinkyo in their endless pursuit of truth. She welcomes all who come seeking knowledge, provided they do so with humility and the intent to contribute. To her, knowledge is sacred only when shared; she blesses those who give as much as they receive. Her presence is felt in silent contemplation, the rustle of turning pages, and the hush of mountain winds through temple libraries. In her eyes, everyone is a student—none exempt from learning.
### **Character:** 
Kyokaku is calm, contemplative, and unfailingly neutral. Though she does not intervene in mortal conflicts, she is **present and attentive**, gently shaping the course of civilization through the spread of information, memory, and insight. She despises ignorance born of arrogance or laziness but rewards diligence, curiosity, and open-mindedness. While she rarely speaks, when she does—through dreams, omens, or sudden moments of realization—her words carry the weight of divine truth.
### **Symbolism:** 
Clarity, shared wisdom, the stillness of high places, open-mindedness, the breath between thought and speech.
