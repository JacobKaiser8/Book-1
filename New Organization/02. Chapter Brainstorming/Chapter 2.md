## Main Goal: 

- The main goal of this chapter is to establish fear of actual conflict in [[Marcus]]. 
	- [[Marcus]] will learn that the world is more brutal and unforgiving than he thought
	- This is the beginning of him feeling helpless so that he can overcome it later
- This chapter will introduce the Iskarm into the story.
	- This chapter give [[Marcus]] a reason to hate the Iskarm for when he is kidnapped later. More than just being kidnapped
	- This will also introduce a lower level Pactorii - [[Köldari]]
- This will begin the vision of fire being destructive.
- ARC: Beginning of the city of [[Monteverra]], before the eruption
## Setting and Atmosphere:

- The atmosphere is fully of panic and people dying
