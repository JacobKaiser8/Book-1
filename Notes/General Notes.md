# Things Not to Do 

Explanation addiction - Over explaining when the reader gets it. Let the actions and context of the situation create feeling
- With each emotion word ask, "did I already explain this?" Trust the  reader to be intelligent.

Talking Heads Problem - Dialog that sounds like a therapy session rather than a real conversation
- Make the conversation have emotion rather than the words be the emotion

Point of View Drift - Stay in one character's head rather than moving between the two - One Scene One Head

Stakes that Never Land - Make sure there is a human element to the stakes. The thing that cannot be undone

Pacing Plateau - Everything is the same pace. Add some difference to the pace of the wording

Start where the story starts

Overwritting - Spread out the descriptions.
